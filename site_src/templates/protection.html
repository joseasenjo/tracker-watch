How this test works, and what it does not say

  • We download a public privacy filter list when the scan runs (it is not stored in our repository) and apply only its simplest rules: whole domains, optionally limited to third-party requests or to some resource types. Rules with paths, wildcards, page elements or site-specific conditions are skipped. A real blocker using the full list would usually block more.
  • Blocked requests are recorded and aborted. “With” counts what still went out. Tracking services are still counted with our own classification list, which is independent from the filter list.
  • The two measurements are taken at different moments (up to a week apart), so ordinary run-to-run variation is part of the difference. Each figure is a median of several passes.
  • Blocking can change how a page works (video, comments, paywalls). We do not measure that.
  • Many browsers and extensions use lists like this one. Naming the list is not an endorsement of any product, and we receive nothing from any of them.
  • This test is separate from the weekly ranking and never changes it. Raw reports: data/protected/<origin>/<date>/; table: protection.csv.
