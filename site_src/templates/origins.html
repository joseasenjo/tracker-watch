How to read this

  • The measurements were taken from: {% for o in origins.origins %}{{ o }} ({{ origins.dates[o]|join(', ') }}){% if not loop.last %}; {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Each origin is one machine on one network, at one moment.
  • Consent tool says whether a consent-management service in our list was contacted. One possible cause of a gap is a consent wall that appears in some countries and not in others, but this page does not prove that is the cause: our list of consent tools is small, so “not seen” does not mean “absent”, and the column does not line up consistently with the gaps.
  • Other possible causes: advertising and tests that differ by country, different content delivery paths, and ordinary run-to-run variation. Each figure is a median of several passes, which reduces but does not remove the last one.
  • A gap is not a verdict on any site. It shows that a single number depends on the vantage point.

More origins can be added by running the scanner from another place and saving its reports under data/extra/<origin>/ (see the data section).
