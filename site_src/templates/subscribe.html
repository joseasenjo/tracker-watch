Weekly email

A short email after each weekly check, only if you ask for it. No account, no tracking, and you can leave at any time.

What you get

  • The weekly summary: the same text we publish on Bluesky after each Monday check (counts of the third-party tracking services contacted by the news sites we follow, what changed, and the method).
  • A short update on Wednesdays, when there is something to say.
  • At the end, a line from the developer: if your organisation needs a tracking and consent audit of its websites, a security review or an automation, you can write back and talk it through. It is an offer, never a requirement.

How it works

Ask

Fill in your address below and tick the box. Nothing else is needed.

Confirm

We reply asking you to confirm that you want it. You confirm by replying. Nothing is sent before that.

Receive, or leave

You get the email after each published check. To stop, reply UNSUBSCRIBE to any of them: you are removed at once.
