How to read this

  • Median per scan: the middle value of the tracking services each site contacted before any click. A scan with fewer sites measured is noted in the table; early scans did not include every country.
  • Leading company reach: on how many of the measured sites a company's tracking services ran in every stable pass. It names companies, never outlets. It is the same count as on the Companies page, where the leader's figure is explained service by service.
  • Concentration index: for each scan, a company's share is its presences divided by all presences on the measured sites; the index is the sum of the squared shares times 10,000 (the Herfindahl-Hirschman form). 10,000 would be a single company present everywhere; a low number means the presence is spread over many. It describes how concentrated the presence on these news sites is. It is not an economic market share and says nothing about what any company does with the data.
  • Limits: all figures come from the same measurement as the rest of the site, from one origin and with a limited classification list, so they are minimums. They do not show intent or legality. See the method.
