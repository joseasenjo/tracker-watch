Sites we could not measure, and why

The tool identifies itself and does not try to bypass blocks, so some sites refuse it. We list them instead of hiding them.

A limit of this study: the outlets that refuse an automated browser are exactly the ones we cannot describe, so the figures lean towards outlets that do not block. Some sites have refused every scan since October (a 403 or 401 answer, or a page that never loads). We keep them on the list and say so here instead of dropping them, and we do not try to get around the refusal. A site that refused Monday's scan is asked once more the next day, from another machine (method). The list of outlets is reviewed at the start of a month, and when we replace any, the change is announced here.
